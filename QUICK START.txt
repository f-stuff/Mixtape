MIXTAPE - QUICK START
===============================================
Mixtape v1.4.1  |  Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit

GET STARTED
-----------
1. Extract the ZIP into a folder (for example, your Desktop).
2. Double-click Mixtape.exe.
3. Mixtape opens in your Windows default browser (including Firefox).
4. Search for an artist, choose the correct artist, tick albums, and
   click Add to queue. You can also paste YouTube or YouTube Music
   video and playlist links in the Paste links screen.
5. Leave Mixtape running. Your songs arrive in the Music folder.

CHOOSING SONGS INSIDE AN ALBUM
------------------------------
Open My queue and expand an album (new albums open their song lists
automatically). Uncheck any songs you don't want downloaded.
Mixtape remembers those as Skipped, even after a restart. Check a song
again to put it back in the download queue. If a song is already saved,
the checkbox is disabled; no downloaded MP3s are deleted.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
--------------------------
Mixtape downloads three free helper tools automatically: yt-dlp,
FFmpeg and Deno. No Python, terminal, installer, or administrator
permission is needed. This first-time setup DOES require an Internet
connection and may download around 170 MB. The screen shows progress.
After that, these helpers are cached. You will still need Internet
access for music searches and downloads.

WHERE YOUR MP3S GO
------------------
Default location: your Windows Music folder, inside a Mixtape folder.

For albums:
  Music\Mixtape\Artist\Album (Year)\01 - Song Name.mp3

For links:
  Music\Mixtape\From Links\Video Title [video-id].mp3

Use Settings > Browse... to choose a music folder, then click Save settings.
You can also paste a path into the box.

HOW TO KEEP IT RUNNING
----------------------
You may close the Mixtape window while a download is running.
Selected songs continue downloading in the tray. Windows can show
"Downloading in background" when you close the window, followed by
"Done" when the batch finishes (subject to notification settings).
You can then reopen the window and close it again to exit Mixtape.
If the last window is closed when no download is active (for example,
six tracks saved and six unchecked), Mixtape exits automatically after
about 3 seconds. Skipped, failed, or waiting tracks remain saved.
Paused queues can close normally; the PC must stay on for downloads.
The tray tooltip and menu status match the webpage (e.g. Finished).
If Firefox misses both close requests, the fallback checks for an
abandoned browser tab after 30 seconds, then allows the closing grace.
Click a notification or left-click the tray icon to reopen Mixtape,
or right-click the tray icon for
Open Mixtape, Open music folder, and Quit Mixtape. It may appear under
the Windows taskbar's hidden-icons arrow. You can also double-click
Mixtape.exe again to reopen the page. To stop it completely, use
Quit Mixtape from the tray menu or Settings. Open tabs detect that the
backend has quit and attempt to close themselves. Firefox generally blocks
script-closing tabs opened by an external app; if that happens Mixtape shows
"Mixtape is closed" instead. Close that tab manually. Other tabs are safe.

KEEP MIXTAPE UP TO DATE
-----------------------
Mixtape checks the public f-stuff/Mixtape GitHub Releases page automatically.
When a newer version is available, click Update & restart. It downloads
and verifies the ZIP, applies it after the current app closes, and reopens.
Downloads must be idle (or the queue paused) before installing. Music files,
settings and your saved queue are kept. You can also check from
Settings > Mixtape updates > Check for updates.

PAUSING DOWNLOADS
-----------------
Choose Pause queue in My queue to stop new downloads from starting.
Any song currently downloading is allowed to finish. Choose Resume queue
to continue. Mixtape remembers the pause setting after a restart.

IF A SONG NEEDS REVIEW
----------------------
Sometimes a YouTube result is a cover, a live performance, or otherwise
uncertain. Mixtape will flag it in the queue rather than silently
choose a doubtful recording. Pick a suggested match or paste a known
song link to continue.

IF FIRST-TIME SETUP OR DOWNLOADS FAIL
-------------------------------------
- Check your Internet connection and press Try again.
- In My queue, failed songs, albums and links have an explanation.
  Open Technical details for more information, or press Copy error to
  share a diagnostic report. Review any local paths or URLs first.
- Press Retry failed (N) to retry every failed download.
  Tracks marked Needs review still require you to choose a match.
- Mixtape checks for yt-dlp updates automatically about once a week
  while the queue is idle. Settings > Check now can check immediately.
- Some YouTube videos may be region-limited, unavailable, or require
  sign-in. Mixtape cannot guarantee that every video can be downloaded.
- Artist and album searches automatically retry brief MusicBrainz
  outages a couple of times; you can retry manually if all attempts fail.
- This app was cross-compiled for Windows but not yet tested on a real
  Windows PC; if you encounter a problem, the Source ZIP contains
  the app's full source code.

WINDOWS SECURITY NOTE
---------------------
This is a newly built, unsigned application. Windows may show a
SmartScreen security warning. Only run software you trust and verify
its origin. Mixtape serves a web page only on 127.0.0.1 (your own PC).

DATA AND PRIVACY
----------------
The queue and settings are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mixtape\queue.json.
The downloaded tools are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mixtape\tools.
Music searches contact MusicBrainz and YouTube; cover art comes from
Cover Art Archive. No Mixtape account is required.

Please download and save only recordings you have permission to use.

FINDING DOWNLOADED FILES
------------------------
In the queue, choose Show file beside a finished song to select its MP3
in Explorer. Choose Open folder beside an album with completed songs to
open that album's directory. For playlist links, Open folder shows the
From Links directory.
