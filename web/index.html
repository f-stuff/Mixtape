Quit Mixtape

Closing the last tab while idle exits Mixtape. If you close during downloads, the batch finishes in the tray; reopen and close the page afterward to exit. Quit here or from the tray menu to stop immediately.

Use Mixtape to download audio you're authorised to save. Music information comes from MusicBrainz; album images from Cover Art Archive. The downloader is yt-dlp, with FFmpeg and Deno. Audio quality can never exceed the original recording.
